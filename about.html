Why a .org?

They told me in middle school that .org websites are the safest. They said if it ends in .org, you can trust it. So I bought the most untrustworthy-sounding domain name I could think of and put it on a .org just to see if the filter would let it through.

It turns out, "safety" is just a label. This site is my personal space. It's not a platform for the masses, and it's not a "Shadow IT" operation for 300 users. It's just me, my receipts, and the things that made me say "s***" in the middle of a Tuesday.

The Philosophy

I spent years building "V-Suites" and "Ecosystems" that looked like they belonged to a Silicon Valley startup. I realized I was just following a template.

Things and s*** is the pivot to reality. It's blocky, it's loud, and it's not trying to be professional. It's an archive of adrenaline crashes and the physical items that remind me I'm still alive.

Local First

Unlike 4SP, where everything is synced and shared, the "My Board" section here is yours only. It lives in your browser's IndexedDB. If you clear your cache, it's gone. That's the point. Some things aren't meant to be in the cloud forever.
