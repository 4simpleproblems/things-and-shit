pet peeves.

Things that make me want to leave the room immediately.
  • Skin Squeaking
    When someone drags their skin across a desk or any flat surface and it makes that high-pitched squeaking noise. The feeling is horrible. It's like nails on a chalkboard but worse.
  • Spit Noises
    When people make loud, wet noises with their spit while talking or just existing. Ewww. Immediate exit from the conversation.
  • Web Restrictions
    Websites that disable right-click or try to stop you from viewing the source. It doesn't work, and it's just annoying.
